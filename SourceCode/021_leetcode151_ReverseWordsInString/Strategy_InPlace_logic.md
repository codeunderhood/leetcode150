# 💡 Core In-Place Idea

We do 3 major steps:  

## STEP 1 → Reverse Entire String

Example:  

"the sky is blue"  

After full reverse:  

"eulb si yks eht"  
## STEP 2 → Reverse Each Word
"blue is sky the"  

Now words become correct.  

## STEP 3 → Remove Extra Spaces

Convert:  

"  blue   is sky   the  "  

into:  

"blue is sky the"
